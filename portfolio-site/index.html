About

From software to the power grid

I'm a Brazilian electrical engineering student, currently in Toulouse for a double degree between the Federal University of Santa Catarina (UFSC) and INP-ENSEEIHT, where I follow the 3EA department's Energy track.

My path started with a technical degree in systems development, so software is part of how I approach engineering problems. At UFSC's LABSPOT lab I work on distribution networks with distributed generation: simulating them in OpenDSS and forecasting their load with machine learning.

Next, I want to go deeper into power electronics for grid integration: grid-connected inverters, their control, and the electrical studies that decide how they connect to the network.

Brazilian and Italian citizen, so no visa is needed to work in the EU.
